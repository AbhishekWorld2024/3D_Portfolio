# Abhishek Arugonda — Weekly Schedule & Lifestyle

## Overview
Abhishek's weekly routine balances fitness, focused work, learning, and social
life. Mornings usually start between 6:30–8:30 AM (gym, run, or yoga), core work
happens late morning through afternoon with a stand-up call around 9:00 AM, and
evenings mix hobbies, friends/family, and rest. He works a hybrid/office
schedule with daily stand-up calls and protects time for personal projects and
certification study. General availability for work is during standard daytime
hours on weekdays.

## Monday — Gym + Work + Friends Night
Wake 7:00 AM; gym 7:30–8:30 AM; stand-up call 9:00 AM; work 10:00 AM–12:00 PM;
cooking 12:00–1:00 PM; work 1:00–3:00 PM; nap 3:00 PM; out with friends and
dinner from 5:00 PM; home 10:00 PM; sleep 11:00 PM.

## Tuesday — Run + Deep Work + Errands
Wake 6:30 AM and morning run; breakfast at home 7:30 AM; stand-up 9:00 AM; deep
focus blocks 9:30 AM; lunch at desk 12:30 PM; work until 4:00 PM; errands (bank,
pharmacy, grocery) 4:00 PM; read/podcast 6:00 PM; cook dinner 7:30 PM; movie
9:00 PM; sleep 10:30 PM.

## Wednesday — Yoga + Meetings + Hobby
Wake 7:30 AM; yoga/stretch 8:00 AM; stand-up 9:00 AM; meetings until 12:00 PM;
team lunch 12:00 PM; work until 4:00 PM; certification study 4:00 PM; hobby
(gaming/music/coding side project) 7:00 PM; light dinner 9:00 PM; sleep 11:00 PM.

## Thursday — Gym Strength + Sports
Wake 7:00 AM; strength gym 7:45–8:45 AM; stand-up 9:00 AM; work 10:00 AM–3:00 PM
with lunch; nap 3:00 PM; sports (badminton/cricket/football) 4:30 PM; dinner with
friends 7:00 PM; home 9:30 PM; sleep 11:00 PM.

## Friday — Gym + Early Logoff + Night Out
Wake 7:00 AM; gym 7:30–8:30 AM; stand-up 9:00 AM; wrap up the week and clear
backlog; lunch with colleagues 12:00 PM; log off early ~3:00 PM; relax; dinner
or hangout with friends 7:30 PM; home 11:00 PM; sleep 12:00 AM.

## Saturday — Chores + Outing + Personal Project
Wake 8:00 AM; slow breakfast; chores (laundry, cleaning) 10:00 AM; lunch at home
12:00 PM; out (market, mall, or long drive) 1:00 PM; personal project/hobby
6:30 PM; dinner out 8:30 PM; home 10:30 PM; sleep 11:30 PM.

## Sunday — Rest + Family + Meal Prep
Wake 8:30 AM; brunch; grocery and weekly planning 10:00 AM; rest 1:00 PM; family
video call 4:00 PM; meal prep for Monday 5:30 PM; light dinner 7:00 PM; plan the
week 8:00 PM; early sleep 10:00 PM.
